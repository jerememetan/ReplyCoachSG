TRAINING DATA QUALITY REVIEW — 30 examples

Start with review/batch_01.csv (10 examples). Review either the three batch files or all_candidates.csv, not both.

For each row, fill decision: good / bad / unsure. Use corrected_standard and corrected_local for edits. Mark good only after you accept the complete pair. Leave unseen rows blank. Keep the first six columns unchanged.

Check: context facts; speaker direction; English/LOCAL decision and meaning; natural Singlish. Accept different sensible intentions. A refusal or counterproposal need not match an imagined reference intent. Do not force lah/leh/Can into every reply. Distinguish a reasonable selectable preference/commitment from an invented external fact or contradiction.

assistant_suggestions.csv contains optional assistant findings and proposed edits, not your decisions. Nothing is pre-approved or automatically corrected. original_target_comparison.csv shows the original and expanded versions for the10 original items.

Selection:10 rewritten original items,10 generated fresh100 items,10 generated volume500 items; deterministic seed1120. Only training data; validation inputs excluded. This sample is a curation exercise, not a model accuracy test or a certification of the other760 items.

Reply in chat with example numbers and edits if easier; your explicit decisions can be entered into the review file. Once reviewed, the existing human_expansion_review.py import command can export an approved candidate subset with provenance. It does not launch training or overwrite a dataset.

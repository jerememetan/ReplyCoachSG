revisions
Fresh-reader editorial simulation by the same assistant; not an independent blinded reviewer,
a human grade or a new model evaluation.



Overall assessment
The section has a coherent research story and useful evidence. Its main editorial weaknesses
are unclear experiment boundaries, duplicated explanations and dense tables. Several claims
also need tighter separation between observed results and plausible explanations.



What already works
   Clear progression from mT5 adaptation to meaning-preserving rewriting, larger teachers
    and compression.
   Concrete datasets, settings, values and failure examples make the work substantive.
   Appropriateness, local style and paired meaning are treated as separate objectives.
   The latest distillation results use matched inputs and metrics and report uncertainty.
   The account is candid about persistent errors and does not claim that every experiment
    succeeded.

Research gaps requiring further evidence
   No clean ablation removes only the original public-adaptation stage, so its necessity and
    isolated effect remain unmeasured.
   The two teachers have not received the same matched judge scoring as the five students; a
    numerical teacher–student quality gap cannot be reported.
   The dataset audit is descriptive; it does not establish the causal contribution of synthetic
    proportion, target style, decoding or regional pretraining.
   The current judge schema does not measure English–Singlish consistency jointly, and no
    new blinded human local-style study has been run.
   The repeated 50-conversation validation results do not establish untouched test
    performance or broad statistical reliability.
   Varying teacher models and adding KD alignment components do not isolate regional
    pretraining or a single distillation loss term.

The review recommendations above are editorial judgments. No missing experiment, human
assessment or teacher score has been fabricated to close a research gap.

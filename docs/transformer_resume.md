- Original pipeline: mT5-base, Singlish span adaptation, public dialogue training, then task LoRA. A larger instruction-tuned model is a candidate, not proof that mT5 is intrinsically unsuitable or simply too small.
- Shared STANDARD-to-LOCAL conditioning improved cross-style meaning in three-seed assistant development reviews, while contextual adequacy barely changed. See `experiments/shared_intent_v1.md`.
- Synthetic expansions, target distillation, targeted contrasts and broader adapters did not establish a reliable overall upgrade. More examples or more epochs are not demonstrated fixes.
- Full STANDARD fine-tuning on 790 conversations overfit. High-rate epoch 1 validation loss was 2.1106; its old epoch 8 endpoint had train loss 0.0334 and validation loss 3.7708. Retain early validation-selected checkpoints. See `experiments/standard_val_v1.md`.
- User paired review: hybrid 5 wins, earlier paired 5, tie 1, both bad 19 of 30. Unnatural Can openings and English copying were major concerns. See `experiments/hybrid_pair_ab_v1_results.json`.
- Curated targets increased interjection Can openings from 27/190 to 48/190. This is a plausible contributor to collapse, not a proven sole cause. Preserve natural original LOCAL wording and faithful English anchors.
- Native rewrite v2 jobs 355353/355354/355355 completed. Best validation losses were 2.4064 context, 2.3863 anchor-only and 2.4219 exemplar. Outputs still copied English and changed meaning. See `experiments/native_rewrite_v2_findings.md`.
- Diagnostic 355463 completed: all 24 training LOCAL targets reproduced by step 100, while author-heldout validation loss later rose to 5.773 at step 300. This deliberately memorizing test demonstrates fitting capacity, not generalization. Meaning probes still failed. See `experiments/rewrite_diagnostic_v3_results.json`.
- Human-preference DPO pilot had unchanged heldout ranking 8/17; no demonstrated fresh reply gain. Prompted reward scorers failed on actual outputs. Structured action RL did not establish a conversation improvement. Do not restart RL or letter-choice detours without new evidence.
